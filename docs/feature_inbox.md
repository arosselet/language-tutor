# Feature Inbox

Proposals that need the learner's yes or no. The diagnosis pass and the tutor's escalation
write here (`protocol/diagnosis.md`, `protocol/toolbelt.md`); `@build` reads it. One entry
per itch: the date, what hurts, the evidence (a feedback-ledger row, a log line), and the
smallest change that would answer it. Nothing here is built until the learner says yes;
an accepted entry moves to `docs/DECISIONS.md` with its commit, and a rejected one is
deleted with a one-line reason in the commit.
