# Which channel carries a dose — capacity routes, curriculum fills

Standing audio authority and payload choices live in `protocol/commissioning.md`.
**Capacity guides the channel choice; it never gates production.** Use known context and
judgment, including preparation for later listening. No capacity question is required
before rendering.

| The learner's situation | Channel | Command |
|---|---|---|
| Wiped out — nothing left to follow | **soak loop** (flat repetition) | `python scripts/render_soak.py` |
| Ears only, mouth busy, but can hold a thread | **rotation** (movements: patterns, scenes, eavesdrop, **lore**) | `python scripts/render_rotation.py` |
| Alone in the car, dishes, a solo walk — mouth free | **drill track** | `python scripts/render_drill.py` |
| Sitting down, ready to be taught | **episode** | `python scripts/run_studio.py` |

Soak and rotation share a capacity row; **shape** separates them, never length. A long
journey is `--minutes 45`, an ordinary day the default — and lore rides in rotation and
the episode's forms, so routing every tired day to soak silently drops it.

**For repair, the error informs the format.** Match the listening situation and what
actually broke — never the bare fact that two words sound alike:

| What went wrong | Format |
|---|---|
| Can't hear the two apart | **soak** — contrast, then the chunk |
| Hears them fine; the mouth takes the wrong one | **episode** — the word doing work under load |
| Has it, just slow | **drill** — the silence is the demand |

- **The same mistake after a heard dose is that format's answer.** Change format; never
  loop harder — the ledger flags ESCALATE only after a listen. Context is what makes a
  word stick; a collision is not a standing order for repetition.
- **A drill's silence is a demand.** The gap only works if the learner can speak out
  loud; in company it is dead air.
- **An episode is the most expensive and demanding dose** — it earns its place when there
  is attention to spend.
- **"Longer" is not a channel; length is a consequence.** A tired ear asking for longer
  wants more repetition, not more scene — rotation's `--minutes`, never a stretched episode.
- **Host:** the cloud sends knocks and memos; every format here renders where a local
  agent and TTS credentials exist.
- Missing context: choose a useful format; ask only when helpful, never as permission.
