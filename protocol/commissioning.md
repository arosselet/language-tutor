# Commissioning a dose — what it carries

The soak order decides *what* a dose carries; this file owns that question alone. Which
channel carries it is `protocol/audio_channels.md`.

## The tutor owns the commission

**The tutor is free to commission and produce any kind of audio at any time.** No repair,
learner request, permission question or capacity answer is required. Choose for
comprehension, curiosity, enjoyment or repair; a **seed order** may point forward even
while slips remain open. Publication safety still applies.

Consult the **live slips** — `sync_state.py slips` spans every lane — when choosing a
treatment. The learner never has to ask for useful audio.

- **A persistent collision may merit its own order.** One appearance in a three-item tape
  is a mention, not a dose. This sets the dose's SCOPE, not its format.
- **A diagnosed gap deserves attention**, without indefinitely outranking discovery.
- **Commissioning nothing is a first-class outcome.** The close *names* a live pattern
  carrying no dose and moves on; it never refuses. `--slip-commissioned <tag>` books one,
  `--no-commission '<why>'` puts the reasoning on the record. The debt is the tutor's to
  judge, not Python's to collect.

Without this law forward pull wins by default, and the learner ends up having to ask for
the dose that would have helped.
